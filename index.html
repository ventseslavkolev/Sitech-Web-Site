Two materials that hide their problems

Moisture damage in timber and strength development in concrete both happen out of sight and on their own schedule. A sensor inside the material turns both into a measured record.

Wood moisture inside the member

Decay starts above 20 % wood moisture and is typically found years later, when remediation costs run into six figures. SNTH sensors in Ø24 mm holes measure temperature and humidity inside the timber. The platform converts the readings to moisture content for the species, warns at 18 % and keeps the record insurers and certifiers ask for.

Reference: timber lattice tower74 positions instrumented, 62 sensors reporting every 10 minutes since September 2026. Median wood moisture 10.7 %, no sensor above the 18 % watch threshold, data completeness above 93 %.

Timber monitoring

Curing and drying, measured from inside the pour

Stripping, post-tensioning and flooring usually wait for the calendar, not for the concrete. A sensor cast into the fresh pour reports temperature and humidity from inside the slab for months. The platform computes maturity and strength by the equivalent-age method (ASTM C1074) and detects the two points that matter: when the pore structure has stabilised and when intensive hydration is over.

C35/45 slab, Bulgaria, 2026Sensor cast in on 21 May. Humidity fell from saturation to 90 %; the strength estimate reached 97.5 % of the design value after three and a half months. Radio through the reinforced slab at RSSI 82–103.
