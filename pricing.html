From drawing to report

A 50-point network is planned, installed and verified in one working day. This is the sequence.

  1. 1

    Coverage plan

    Sensor positions and gateway location on your drawing, expected signal strength, a second gateway where needed.

  2. 2

    Installation

    Ø24 mm hole 45 mm deep, cast-in or free placement; gateway with power or solar, SIM and APN. About 50 points per day.

  3. 3

    Commissioning

    Sensors registered by ID and named by position; groups, thresholds and recipients set; first packets within ten minutes.

  4. 4

    Training and support

    One-hour online session; e-mail and phone Monday to Friday 9–18 CET; critical response within four hours.

  5. 5

    Analysis reports

    Weekly or monthly per position: trends, extremes, offsets, outages, link quality, wood moisture or maturity.

  6. 6

    Lifecycle

    24-month warranty, 10-year sensor battery, hardware re-usable on the next object, data exportable at any time.
