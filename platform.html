sensornetwork.cloud

See it, alarm it, export it.

The platform runs in the browser on a PC, tablet or phone. Users and roles are set per customer; alarms go out by SMS, e-mail and push; every reading can be exported. There is nothing to install.

Log inAsk for a demo account
Dashboard
Current value, sparkline, battery, signal strength and last-seen time per sensor, grouped by room, element or building. Colours and thresholds are set by the user.
Alarms
Minimum and maximum per quantity, low battery, weak signal and missing data, per sensor or per group. Recipients receive SMS, e-mail or push notifications; the alarm log keeps the history.
Sensor readouts
Charts over any period with several sensors on one axis, temperature and humidity together, zoom and a date range picker. One click exports the selection to CSV or XLSX.
Wood moisture
Moisture content per sensor computed from temperature and humidity with a species-specific model; watch and alert bands at 18 and 20 %; species selectable per group.
Concrete maturity and drying
Equivalent-age maturity (ASTM C1074) with the parameters of the mix, estimated strength, and automatic detection of the temperature-invariance and desaturation points, summarised in an automatic report.
Gateways and data quality
Gateway log, ambient temperature and air pressure at the gateway, and per-packet sequence numbers and signal strength, so link problems and sensor problems can be told apart.
Reports
Automatic system reports from the platform, and weekly or monthly analysis reports per position written by our engineers as a service.
Administration and API
Sensors, gateways, groups, alarm settings and users per customer. An API for your own systems is available on request.

What the subscription covers

Software and maintenance cost per gateway per month: the cloud platform, alarms, exports, software updates and support by e-mail and phone, Monday to Friday 9–18 CET, with a response to critical problems within four hours. The Demo Set includes the first three months. Prepaid subscriptions are discounted by 2 % for six months, 4 % for twelve and 8 % for twenty-four.

Your data stays yours: the full history is exportable at any time, and sensors and gateways can be moved to the next object.
